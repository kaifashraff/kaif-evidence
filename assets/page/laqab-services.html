The Craft

Every LAQAB piece begins in a 300-year-old tradition — hand-guided needles, real metal threads, and the unhurried patience of master artisans who have embroidered for generations.

Zari & Zardozi

Genuine gold and silver-plated threads, hand-coiled and couched onto silk and velvet. Each motif takes days — never machine-mimicked.

Handwork & Silai

Traditional hand-stitching and finishing techniques that keep every seam structural, every drape intentional, every garment heirloom-grade.

Dyework & Textiles

Seasoned dyeing and fabric sourcing — from Banarasi silks to organza and raw silk — curated to your trousseau's palette.

Bespoke Fitting

Measurements guided by our fit process — in person or via guided video consultation for clients across India and the Gulf.
