# NEET-UG 2026 Paper Leak — Voiceover Script Hinglish

**Purpose:** Kaif ki awaaz mein record karne ke liye clean voiceover script.  
**Style:** Investigative documentary, Hinglish/Roman Urdu, calm but intense.  
**Estimated runtime:** 18–22 minutes depending on speaking speed.  
**Recording method:** Har segment alag audio clip mein record karo: `VO_01`, `VO_02`, etc. Main baad mein isi segment numbering ke hisaab se video/B-roll sync karunga.  
**Legal safety:** Jahan named people ya investigation ka zikr hai, “reports ke mutabiq”, “allegedly”, “accused”, “CBI probe ke hisaab se” use kiya gaya hai. Is wording ko change mat karna.

# Recording Checklist for Kaif

1. Har segment separately record karo: `VO_01.wav`, `VO_02.wav` … `VO_20.wav`.
2. Mic ke paas stable distance rakho.
3. Background fan/noise band karo.
4. Har segment ke start mein 1 second silence, end mein 1 second silence chhodna.
5. Agar line galat ho jaaye, same segment repeat kar do — main edit mein best take use kar lunga.
6. Delivery style: slow, serious, investigative. Angry nahi, controlled intensity.

# Video Sync Notes for Editor/AI Video Build

- Har VO segment ke corresponding B-roll alag banana.
- Confirmed facts ke liye green label: “Reported / Source: News18, Indian Express, India TV…”
- Allegations ke liye yellow label: “Alleged / Under Investigation”.
- Named accused ke visuals mein real face use karne se pehle legal check; safer option: blurred silhouette + article headline.
- Telegram scam segment mein recreated UI use karna, real scam channel details blur karna.
- Emotional student shots generic/stock hone chahiye; kisi real student ko falsely imply na karein.
